Privacy Policy for Text2Code

Last updated: 30 April 2026

Text2Code is an Android app for capturing printed text with the device camera and generating a QR code from the selected text.

Information We Collect

Text2Code does not collect, upload, sell, or share personal information. Camera images are processed on your device for text recognition.

Camera Permission

Text2Code requests camera permission so you can point the camera at printed text, capture it, and select the detected text. Camera access is used only for this app feature.

Local Storage

Text2Code may save recently generated barcode text on your device so you can view recent barcodes again. This recent history stays on your device.

Internet Access

Text2Code does not require an internet connection for its main text capture and QR code features.

Third Parties

Text2Code does not send camera images or selected text to third-party services.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the app developer through the contact details provided on the Google Play listing.
